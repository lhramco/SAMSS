Definitions & Educational Core Functions

Faculty of Alternative Medicine

Definition

Alternative Medicine refers to non-allopathic diagnostic and healing sciences including Electrohomeopathy, Acupuncture, Hydrotherapy, Chromotherapy, Aromatherapy, and Naturopathy that aim to activate self-healing mechanisms and restore bio-energetic balance.

Primary Functions

  • Holistic Patient Assessment: Addressing constitutional, physical, and cellular vitality.
  • Natural Spagyric Therapeutics: Formulating non-toxic remedies from plant essences.
  • Preventative Vitality Restoration: Enhancing metabolic immunity naturally.

Faculty of Social Sciences & Humanities

Definition

Social Sciences represent academic disciplines that analyze governance, economic structures, international dynamics, legal frameworks, history, human behavior, languages, and natural physical sciences.

Primary Functions

  • Governance & Policy Formulation: Training future administrators, legal experts, and diplomats.
  • Socio-Economic Analytical Excellence: Conducting research in economics, statistics, and urban planning.
  • Cultural & Linguistic Heritage: Preserving national history and classical/regional languages.
